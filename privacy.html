Captory Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Captory ("the app") is a Windows app that turns audio and video files into text and subtitles. It is developed and published by an independent developer ("we", "us"), shown as the publisher on Captory's Microsoft Store page.

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Captory. The short version: your files and transcripts never leave your computer, and we do not collect any personal data.

1. Your files and transcripts stay on your device

  • Transcription runs entirely on your computer, using speech recognition models stored on your device.
  • The audio and video files you open, and the text, transcripts and subtitles the app creates, are processed and saved only on your computer, in the folders you choose.
  • We never upload, send, store, read or have access to your files or transcripts.

2. We do not collect personal data

  • Captory has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal information.
  • Captory does not include analytics, telemetry, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services, and it does not use cookies.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any information about you, because we do not have any.

3. The only time Captory uses the internet

Captory connects to the internet for one reason only: to download a speech recognition model the first time you use a quality setting (Fast, Balanced or Most accurate).

  • Models are downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), which hosts the official open-source Whisper models.
  • Like any website, Hugging Face receives the technical information needed to deliver the file, such as your IP address. No audio, video, text or personal data is sent. Hugging Face's handling of that technical information is governed by its own privacy policy: https://huggingface.co/privacy
  • Once a model is downloaded, Captory works completely offline.

4. Purchases

If you buy Captory Pro, the purchase is processed entirely by the Microsoft Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Microsoft's handling of your purchase information is described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement

Microsoft may share with publishers aggregated, non-identifying information about app installs, sales and crashes through its Partner Center dashboard. We use this only to improve the app.

5. Data stored on your device

Captory stores the following on your computer only:

  • Downloaded speech recognition models, in the app's local data folder.
  • Your settings (for example, the selected quality and subtitle options).
  • If the app keeps an error log to help with troubleshooting, it stays on your device and is never sent to us automatically. You may choose to share it with us when asking for support.

Uninstalling Captory from Windows removes the models, settings and logs stored in the app's data folder. Transcripts and subtitles you saved elsewhere remain where you saved them.

6. Children

Captory is a general-purpose productivity app. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

7. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold personal data about you, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, export or delete. If you contact us by email for support, we use your message only to reply, and you can ask us at any time to delete that correspondence.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version at this address and update the effective date above. If a future version of Captory ever needs to collect any information, we will explain it here before that version is released.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or about Captory: captory.app@gmail.com
